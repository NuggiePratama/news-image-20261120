One image summing up Friday 20 November 2026's world news: Russia
launches its biggest strike of the month on Kyiv, hitting three Dnipro
bridges, and missiles and a drone land in NATO Poland; Cyclone Akvan
makes landfall in Odisha; Wall Street plunges on AI fears; COP31 ends
with no deal; Britain and the EU agree a post-Brexit reset package;
Iran's Qeshm blast turns out not to be a strike; Cognition AI eyes a
$75bn valuation.

The image is drawn from scratch with Pillow by `generate.py` (no network),
and `main.ts` copies the resulting 1024×1024 PNG to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 generate.py
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
